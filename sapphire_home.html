Our Engagement Models

At Sapphire, we provide full-cycle custom software development services to meet your financial requirements. Our software development team leverages their expertise in delivering process-oriented and high- performance software products for Cloud, Mobile, and Web.

Fixed Price

This model is for projects with well-defined scope and requirements. When Clients know what they want and have fixed requirements, it works the best for small businesses and medium projects with limited or fixed budgets.

Dedicated Hiring

For projects when clients want to have a full-time, dedicated team working on their projects. Works best for projects which require fast scaling or require expert developers to work on a long-term basis.

Time Material

For projects where requirements are dynamic and constantly changing, and scope is not clearly defined. Works the best for When clients need short-term consulting or want to build a proof of concepts.

Buckets Approach

A lot of businesses typically select our bucket approach which allow them for payment convenience once the project is finished and things are in place.
